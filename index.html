I am a Student Researcher at Google DeepMind in Zurich, working on reinforcement learning for LLM post-training. I am also a final-year Ph.D. candidate at Politecnico di Milano, advised by Prof. Nicola Gatti.

Previously, I was a Visiting Ph.D. Researcher at the Machine Intelligence through Decision-making and Interaction (MIDI) Lab at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Prof. Amy Zhang.

My research focuses on generalization in reinforcement learning, particularly unsupervised RL and continual RL, and extending these foundational principles to LLM post-training.
